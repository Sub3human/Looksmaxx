Face Rater (Auto)

Add 2 photos. The app finds all face points by itself. Then it rates you on the PSL scale (4 is the average person). The rating is honest, not nice. Photos stay on your device.

1. Front photo

Look at the camera. No smile. Hair off forehead. Good light on your face.

2. Side photo

Turn your head to the side. Look straight ahead. Ear must show.

This is a strict estimate from shape and skin pixels. It is not the official PSL app. It cannot see hair, eye color, body or smile. Real bone mass cannot be measured from a photo, so the bone score is a guess from face shape. A number does not decide your worth.
